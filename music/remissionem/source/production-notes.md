# Accepted production

The user explicitly accepted the complete Veil chimes mix on 10 September 2026
and requested a Bandcamp entry. They subsequently reported making it public;
the release page was observed in their logged-in Chrome session. That listening verdict belongs to the published release. The current MIDI and
instrument choices remain the same; the new reference-informed master is a
separate listening result.


## Musical decisions

The opening uses 31 chime notes, with a gradual 1.8-second entrance. Layered
modal resonances, a warm lower body and diffuse space provide the requested
ethereal wind-chime quality. The last note-off is near 6.580 seconds. The direct
voice retreats by 7.2 seconds and the space by 9.4 seconds, supporting the kick
entrance near 6.854 seconds and the later Prism pluck voice.

The approved phrase replaced a series of rejected rises that felt too close to
the reference. An intermediate synth phrase supplied useful timing but sounded
thin; that timing was retained and performed with the new chime sound. Those
old sounds are not alternate current choices.

Prism pluck carries 149 notes across the passages around 48–60, 133.7–144 and
198.9–212.6 seconds. It replaces piano that the user rejected in those passages.
The other 287 piano notes retain their performance and Salamander sound. Pulse
chord stabs retain their separately approved rhythmic harmony. Saw arpeggio,
three bass layers, drums and transition effects complete the fourteen parts.

## Source and identity

The former working title was Forgiveness. The production is now named
Remissionem (Setloom Remix); the original-work title remains in reference
provenance and credits. All fourteen MIDI instrument tracks match the accepted
performance. Renaming changed the conductor title, not musical events.

The released master remains stereo 44.1 kHz PCM24, approximately -13.32 LUFS and
-1.00 dBTP (four-times-oversampled estimate). The 320 kbps MP3 is encoded directly
from this WAV with no gain or mix change. WAV and cover identities were preserved
during cleanup. Rebuild and codec measurements are recorded in the release `qa/`.

Earlier piano and chord-stab studies were removed at the user's request after
the accepted source and stems were consolidated and verified. Superseded mixes
and opening-voice experiments were also removed. All current render inputs are
retained in this production and its independent instrument runtime.
Future musical changes belong in a separate candidate.
