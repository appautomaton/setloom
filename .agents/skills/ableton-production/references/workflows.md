# Ableton production workflows

Use the current brief and performance to choose the next operation. These are
working considerations, not mandatory stages or musical templates. Use the
current MCP schema for Live payloads and units.

## Arrangement and performance

- Inspect the current material and preserve work outside the commissioned edit.
  Give added parts a musical role and consider how they enter, interact, and leave.
- Preserve recognizable motifs when they matter to the brief; compose their
  development and the transitions between sections rather than extending a loop
  merely to fill duration.
- Choose note length, touch, density, accents, and timing from the intended
  gesture. Quantization and timing variation should solve a heard or demonstrated
  problem; neither is a general substitute for phrasing.
- For an instrument swap, inspect actual controller and envelope response. Use
  [reconstruction guidance](../../../../docs/reconstruction.md#instrument-reinterpretation)
  to distinguish performance translation from additional composition.
- In Live, select available browser instruments using returned URIs. If MCP lacks
  a needed operation, inspect other authorized controls; do not invent endpoints
  or make the human operate the studio by default.

## Mix and delivery

- Diagnose the supplied symptom in the current audio, tracks, effects, and routing.
  Similar sounds may span several layers; removing a named track is not proof
  that the audible problem is resolved.
- Choose EQ, dynamics, space, and balance for the material. Compare at useful
  levels and check how a local fix affects the whole passage.
- Complete mix or master work within the brief. Check export integrity, required
  assets, clipping, low-end/mono behavior, and intended dynamics as appropriate.
- Preserve editable performance, settings, and artifact identity. Honor playback
  preferences and follow [working stages](../../../../docs/workflow.md#working-stages)
  for temporary auditions and promotion.
