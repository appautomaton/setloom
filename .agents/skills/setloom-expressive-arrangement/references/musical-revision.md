# Musical revision

## Intent and intervention

Distinguish the character to preserve, the desired listening effect and the means
chosen to achieve it. If the user asks why, explain the cause before treating the
question as an edit request. Scope preferences to the brief; a past solution is
an example, not the next song's arrangement.

Evaluate transposition, reharmonization, rhythmic development or sound design by
the relationships they change. When an effect is weak, locate the cause in the
score, articulation, instrument response or mix before changing the wrong layer.

## Relationships across the form

A line's function depends on the sounding bass, voicing and neighboring parts.
Check held notes against moving harmony and how phrases hand over attention.
Entrances, withdrawal, register, density and spatial tails can develop the scene;
making every part more prominent can erase its shape.

Move between the scale of a sound, phrase, section and whole song as the question
requires. A convincing loop can become tiring through repetition or fail at a
transition. Develop returns and the ending with the same care as the initial
idea. Added layers and busier automation are not necessary evidence of progress.

## Evaluate the performed result

Compare complete gestures with their surrounding transitions in ensemble; use
solos to diagnose. Match playback loudness for tone or pitch comparisons while
keeping intentional balance changes explicit. Whole-song loudness and stem RMS
alone do not establish local presence, masking or perceived impact.

Inspect the actual render using available modalities. Visual evidence can reveal
missing movement or timing errors; spectral resemblance cannot certify musical
success. Keep technical completion and the artifact's listening verdict distinct.

When research can resolve a concrete question, use producer examples and primary
instrument documentation. Distinguish dated practice, current capability and a
claim about trends; test the useful idea in this music. Carry forward the reason
it helped, with its limits, rather than an artist's preset or parameter recipe.
