# Setloom Reference Reconstruction

Recover a supported musical interpretation within the brief. The original MIDI,
preset and session architecture remain unknown unless supplied.

The primary agent owns source comparison, musical inference, sound design and
fidelity review. Do not delegate these judgments; inspect the underlying passages
directly.

- Use the original mix to judge musical identity, prominence and development
  across the commissioned form. Let musical contribution, not extraction
  strength, guide attention.
- Consolidate estimates into supported musical parts. Distinguish independent
  voices from shared content, instrument layers, modulation and returns; revise
  ownership when evidence changes.
- Choose notes, expressive controls and instruments together to realize each
  gesture, including bass and percussion. Correct notes can still sound wrong.
- Open and inspect original, relevant estimates and rebuilt phrases in time and
  frequency. Check omissions, duplicates and altered gestures in ensemble,
  including transitions and meaningful changes in repeats.
- Locate mismatches in source evidence, interpretation, instrument response or
  mix before choosing a correction. Revise failed explanations rather than
  accumulating compensating layers. Metrics do not establish fidelity.
